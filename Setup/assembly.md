# Assembly

## Duofiller Mono

--![](/static/gloves.png)
<br>

When assembling the filler it's mandatory to use gloves. There might be sharp egdes that can cut you badly and gloves will also prevent fingerprints on the stainless surface.<br>


--![](/static/mono1.png)
<br>
Fasten the tubeholder bracket:<br>
<br>
--![](/static/mono2.png)<br>

Fasten the foam pad on the tubeholder:

--![](/static/mono3.png)

Fit the soft tubes over the stainless tubes. 

--![](/static/mono4.png)

The stainless tubes shall go approximately 10mm into the soft tubes. The thickest stainless tube is the beverage tube, it goes on the right side. It has a thin end, this is the end that goes into the soft tube. The thinnest stainless tube (CO2 tube) has one straight cut end and one bevel cut end. The straight end goes into the soft tube on the left side. Beware of the bevel end, it might be sharp.  :

--![](/static/mono5.png)

Position the tube clamp over the tubes:

--![](/static/mono6.png)

Slide the tubeclamp over the tubeholder bracket. You need to push the tubeclamp from beneath and up and it will slide onto the stainless bracket. The foam pad will be compressed to support the tubes in place.

--![](/static/mono7.png)

If you plan to use with cans, fasten the canholder bracket. If you plan to use only with botttles then this bracket is not necessary.

--![](/static/mono8.png)
